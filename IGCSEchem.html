How points work

Each lesson marked understood: 10 XP. Each reasoning self-check: 15 XP. Each recall activity earns its best reward: 5 XP with help, 10 XP after correcting a mistake, or 20 XP independently. Improving upgrades the reward; repeating the same answer does not add points. Every 100 XP unlocks a level. Mistakes and breaks never deduct recall points.
